Add your sounds

Your recordings join the library with an Uploaded tag. Files stay on this device and work offline.

Browse your files and choose audio recordings, such as MP3 or WAV. Up to 25 MB and 2 minutes per file, mono or stereo. Files your browser cannot read as audio are skipped.

Applies to these files. They’ll appear beside the built-in sounds in this category. You can change each recording’s category later.

Add internet radio

Bring a favourite station into your mix. Use a direct HTTPS audio stream (MP3 or AAC), rather than the station’s website or a playlist.

The station must allow playback through a web audio mixer. Radio needs internet access and starts only when you press Play.
