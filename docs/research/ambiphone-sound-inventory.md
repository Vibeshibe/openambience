# Ambiphone sound inventory

Research date: **2026-09-30**. This inventories **67 selectable channels across eight categories**, with **60 distinct directly credited source recordings across 51 channels**. Several channels combine multiple clips. Radio playlists and the dynamic air-traffic-control clip bank are not counted as individual assets here; they are outside the proposed offline scope.

Sources: [live catalog](https://ambiph.one/), [credits](https://ambiph.one/acknowledgements), and public catalog metadata delivered to browsers (version 1.15.0; exact URL and checksum in the JSON). Source-page license links were independently inspected for every directly credited recording. No Ambiphone app code or sound files were copied into this repository. Playback classifications reflect catalog declarations and were not auditioned.

Download the [CSV](ambiphone-sound-inventory.csv) for spreadsheet use, or read the [JSON](ambiphone-sound-inventory.json) for per-source URLs, license evidence, duration, original format/size where available, review status, and page checksums. The metadata is research material, not an executable app catalog.

These counts include the lab recording under its displayed CC BY 4.0 badge even though its description specifies 3.0; it remains on hold. License labels establish candidate eligibility, not sound quality, a complete attribution chain, or rights to background music captured in a recording. All recordings remain **not downloaded / not auditioned**.

## Reading the inventory

- **Loop:** continuous ambience in Ambiphone. An original source recording may still need trimming and crossfading.
- **Event:** intermittent clips, such as thunder or an owl; saved mixes should retain event intervals as well as volume.
- **Track:** a named music recording; optional downloadable content.
- **Radio/live:** network-dependent channel; deferred from the offline release.
- **No credited source:** the catalog provides no reusable original-source/license link. This does not assert that no permission exists elsewhere.

## Corrections and unresolved provenance

- **Vacuum cleaner:** Ambiphone credits CC BY 4.0; the [original Clearwavsound page](https://freesound.org/people/Clearwavsound/sounds/523445/) specifies **CC BY 3.0**. Record the original-page license.
- **Sci-fi lab:** the [source page](https://freesound.org/people/qubodup/sounds/212025/) displays CC BY 4.0 but its creator description explicitly specifies **CC BY 3.0**, with contributor credits. Resolve before selecting a release asset.
- **Crickets:** the [remix](https://freesound.org/people/Moulaythami/sounds/536930/) has a CC BY 4.0 sound badge and credits ten inputs. Its CC BY-SA link describes a photograph, not the sound. Prefer a simpler CC0 recording for the first pack.
- **Snoring:** the [credited Piggimon recording](https://freesound.org/people/Piggimon/sounds/366365/) is CC BY-NC 4.0. A CC0 replacement is listed in the acquisition plan.
- **Whales:** the [Archive item](https://archive.org/details/WhaleSong_928) carries a Public Domain Mark and attributes NOAA; verify the original agency asset before packaging it. A Public Domain Mark is a status assertion, not a license grant.
- **Radio/arcade/carousel material:** check the rights to any embedded broadcasts or music, even when the field-recording upload is marked CC0.
